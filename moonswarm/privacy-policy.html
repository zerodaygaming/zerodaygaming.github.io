Moonswarm Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026 · Moonswarm

Moonswarm: Lunar Tower Defense ("Moonswarm" or "the game") is published by Zero Day Gaming ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles your information.

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We don't run servers that receive it.
  • Google handles three optional things: rewarded ads, in-app purchases and cloud save through Google Play Games.
  • We don't sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally: levels cleared, upgrades, currencies, relics, settings and similar game state. It stays in the game's private storage on your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Moonswarm shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, for example for +50% rewards or a second chance. There are no forced or banner ads. If you buy the Ad-Free Card, you skip ads entirely. The Commander Pass does not remove ads.

When ads are used, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions and diagnostics. Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, depending on your choices, personalize ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent tools.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy He3 packs, the Commander Pass subscription and the Ad-Free Card. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation (which item, and a purchase token) so it can deliver and restore what you bought. Manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game backs up your progress (the same save described above) to your Google account so you can restore it on a new phone or after reinstalling. For this, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google. It does not receive your Google account email, password or contacts. The backup is stored by Google, not by us.

Cloud save is optional: if you don't sign in, nothing is backed up online.

What we don't do

  • We don't run our own servers that collect your data.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking software beyond what the Google services above include.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data.
  • The game has no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

Deleting your data

Delete your local progress by clearing the game's storage in your phone's settings or uninstalling the game. Delete your cloud save in the Google Play Games app (Settings → delete your Play Games account and data). Purchases stay tied to your Google Play account and can be restored. If you need help, email us.

Children

Moonswarm is made for players 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Security and retention

Connections to Google services are encrypted in transit. Your local save stays on your device until you delete it; your cloud save stays in your Google account until you delete it. Data held by Google is kept under Google's own retention rules.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. We hold no personal data ourselves, so most requests are handled by Google; contact us and we'll point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and mention significant changes in the game's release notes.

Contact

Questions: support@zerodaygaming.com. We use emails only to reply to you.
