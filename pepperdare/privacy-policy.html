Pepperdare Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026 · Terms of Service · Pepperdare

Pepperdare: Truth or Dare ("Pepperdare" or "the app") is a party game for adults, published by Zero Day Gaming ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short, it stays on your phone.

  • Your game data stays on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • We don't share, sell or use your data in any way, because we never have it.

Information stored by the app

To set up a game, you can add players with a name, gender, sexuality and partners. The app saves these players, your spice-level settings, your age confirmation and which card packs you've unlocked. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Information we collect

None. Pepperdare has no user accounts and no servers that receive your information. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We cannot see your data and could not provide it to anyone, because we do not have it.

Device backups

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google backup on Android), your operating system may include the app's settings and saved players in that backup. That backup is handled by your device provider under its own privacy policy, and we can't access it.

Internet use and purchases

The app connects to the internet only to show prices for, buy and restore optional card packs and Unlimited Access through Google Play or the Apple App Store. That exchange is handled by the store under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details, and none of your players or game settings are sent.

Device permissions

  • Network: only for store purchases, as described above.

The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Deleting your data

Remove any player from the app at any time, or clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, deleted data cannot be recovered. Purchases stay tied to your store account and can be restored from Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

Pepperdare contains sexual content and is only for adults 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and the app asks you to confirm your age before you can play.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
